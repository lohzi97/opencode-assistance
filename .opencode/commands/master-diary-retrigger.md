Check in with the Master to see if there is anything worth recording for the diary.

## Instruction

Send a warm, natural message to the Master. Ask some combination of the following questions — pick the ones that feel most natural given the time of day and the tone of your conversation so far. You do not need to ask all of them every time. Vary your phrasing so it does not feel scripted.

- How is your day going so far?
- Is there anything worth writing down?
- How are you feeling?
- How is your energy level today?
- What have you been up to?
- Did you meet anyone interesting today?
- Did you come across anything interesting or noteworthy?

## Tone

Keep it casual and warm, as a friend would ask. Do not fire all questions at once — pick one or two that fit naturally. If the Master has already been chatting, you can simply ask if there is anything else to add.

## Behavior

- If the Master responds, acknowledge naturally and continue the conversation if they want to.
- If the Master says nothing or gives a short "nothing much," acknowledge briefly and let it be. Do not press further.
- Do not write any diary entry. You are only collecting material.
