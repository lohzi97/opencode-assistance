## Output Format

Write a summary with these sections:

### Diary Date

The YYYYMMDD date this session covers.

### Master's Day — Events & Activities

- Bullet points of everything the Master mentioned doing, attending, or experiencing.
- Include people, places, and specific details.

### Master's Thoughts & Feelings

- The Master's emotional state, energy, mood at different points.

### Notable Details

- Interesting things the Master found or encountered.
- Decisions, plans, or intentions mentioned.
- Health, rest, or well-being context.

### Conversation Notes

- Any ongoing topics or threads that were being discussed when rollover happened.
- Anything the Master said they wanted to follow up on later.

- Be descriptive but concise. Every fact matters; fluff does not.
